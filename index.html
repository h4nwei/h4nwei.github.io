Biography

I am a Professor at the School of Computing and Artificial Intelligence, Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics. Before joining JXUFE, I was a Research Scientist at Nanyang Technological University. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from City University of Hong Kong.

My research interests include computer vision, foundation models, world models, and green AI, with particular emphasis on visual quality assessment and efficient intelligent systems.

Research Interests

  • Computer Vision and Visual Quality Assessment
  • Foundation Models and World Models
  • Efficient Learning and Green AI
